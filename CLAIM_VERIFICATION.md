Thus the YAML’s step-level secret mappings are available to its `if`. This checks the claimed mechanism; it does not verify whether the repository’s secrets are configured or any real deployment succeeded. Sources: [GitHub env context](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/contexts#env-context), [official runner StepsRunner.cs](https://github.com/actions/runner/blob/main/src/Runner.Worker/StepsRunner.cs).
